Mid-mandate leads bought little protection
Governing-party margin over its top opponent: day-674 poll → final election result. Straight connectors mark endpoints only — the paths between are not polled at model grade.
Graham (−29.3 pts), Alward (−20.1) and Gallant (−16.1) all gave back their entire day-674 lead and lost office. Gallant even won the popular vote in 2018 — and still lost government on seats.
Higgs is different: minority government, first COVID wave, and an election only 47 days after this point. His −8.0 drift is a short-runway case, not a fixed-date analogue.
Read carefully: history warns that leads decay; it does not decide that Holt’s will. Four cases cannot support a probability.

HYPOTHESIS — NOT A FINDING
The delivery-legibility gap
A possible gap between what a government has announced or implemented, what residents can observe in their daily lives, and what they credit to the government.
{{s.k}}
{{s.n}}
▲ THE CHAIN WEAKENS HERE
WHAT IS OBSERVED
Real outputs — 15 clinics, 7,587 housing starts, universal breakfast — coexist with 25–29% issue-handling approval and only 32% seeing positive change. The gap itself is measured; its mechanism is not.
RIVAL EXPLANATIONS
1 · Credit conversion: outputs aren’t yet felt or attributed. 2 · Mandate compression: six coequal priorities blur the story. 3 · Adequacy: residents see it and judge it insufficient. Current surveys cannot separate these.
WHAT WOULD CONFIRM OR REJECT IT
One survey measuring, in the same respondents: policy awareness → correct attribution → personal experience/adequacy → trust and vote. High awareness + high attribution + negative adequacy would reject the communications framing and point to policy substance instead. Two negative controls already exist: wildfire response earned the highest issue rating, and no broad French-language transmission failure appears in available indicators.
UNOBSERVED
Senior civil-service morale: public evidence does not establish it either way — no representative survey, turnover, vacancy or absence series exists. Treated as a question to measure, not a finding.
