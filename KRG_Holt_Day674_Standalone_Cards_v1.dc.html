STATUS: NOT ESTABLISHED — NOT CODED GREEN, AMBER OR RED
No representative survey, turnover flow, vacancy, sick-leave or confidence series exists in public — so the evidence neither establishes nor rules out a morale problem. The public leadership record shows mostly internal moves and announced retirements. Documented strain mechanisms are a separate question: a 12% workforce-reduction plan, RTIPPA meeting its 30-day clock 59.5% of the time, one Auditor-General staffing case.
What would settle it: quarterly workforce + vacancy + absence series · service standards matched to staffing · a representative anonymous employee survey.
Reported concern is a reason to measure — not a finding to publish.
